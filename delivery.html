Delivery & Payment

Order your bean bag from Puffy, and we’ll make sure it arrives safely right at your door. We deliver across Italy, including Milan and surrounding regions - 7 days a week.
Nationwide shipping is also available to all major cities.

Delivery

We offer delivery of our soft bean bags throughout Italy. If the selected model is in stock, we’ll confirm the delivery date when you place your order. Delivery options are:
Pickup — Free
‍After placing your order, our manager will confirm the nearest pickup location.
Local Home Delivery — Free
‍Free delivery within Milan and up to 30 km across the metropolitan area.
Delivery Across Italy — €9
‍For addresses outside our free local delivery area, home delivery costs €9.
For transportation, each bean bag is carefully wrapped in protective polyethylene film to prevent mechanical damage. Your order arrives safe, clean, and ready to enjoy.

Payment

You can purchase bean bags, balls, and kids poufs directly through our online store and pay in euros (€).We offer the following payment options:
Bank Transfer (for businesses)
‍An invoice will be provided after order confirmation.
Cash on Delivery
‍Pay directly to the courier upon delivery.
Credit / Debit Card
‍Secure online payments via card or payment upon delivery (where available).
Please keep your payment receipt as confirmation.

Returns & Exchanges

In accordance with EU consumer protection regulations, you may return or exchange a product within 14 days of purchase. Please note:
The item must retain its original appearance and consumer properties.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method.
Return shipping costs are covered by the customer unless the item is defective.
