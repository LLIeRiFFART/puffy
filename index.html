Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum weight capacity?
Our adult bean bags comfortably support up to 150 kg (330 lbs) without losing shape or stability.Children’s models and sizes M and L are designed for users up to 75 kg (165 lbs), ensuring proper comfort and durability.
What warranty do you provide?
All our products come with a 3-year manufacturer warranty.

The warranty covers any production-related defects, including stitching issues, hardware faults, fabric defects, or inner liner damage — when used as intended and within the recommended weight limits.

Please note: natural filling compression over time is not covered under warranty.
What filling do you use? Does it have any smell?
We use premium-grade EPS filling, manufactured in Europe from high-quality raw materials.

The granules (1–4 mm) are durable, resistant to excessive compression, odor-free, non-toxic, and hypoallergenic — making them safe for both adults and children.
What if the bean bag doesn’t fit my space? Can I return it?
Yes, of course.You may return or exchange an unused product in its original condition within 14 days of delivery.

We want you to feel completely confident in your purchase.
How often should the filling be refilled?
Over time, the filling may naturally compress depending on usage intensity. This usually happens during the first year.

Refilling is simple and can be done at any time. On average, it requires 70–100 liters to restore the original volume.
